# LinkedIn Job Hunt Tactics

The job hunt phase on LinkedIn includes creating alerts, using specific search filters, and managing profile visibility settings.

## Details

- Create job alerts to receive notifications for new roles matching desired criteria. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Use filters to narrow results by time frame (e.g., past week), experience level, company size, and work type. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Toggle the 'Open to opportunities' button to be visible to recruiters. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Automatically share your profile when applying for a job is an option in settings. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Signal interest to a company by sharing job alerts and signaling interest to recruiters. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
