# Successful Job Hunt Components

A successful job hunt involves five key elements: exploration, research, positioning, networking, and the job hunt itself.

## Details

- Exploration involves navigating the alumni tool to search by school name and filter by desired function or company. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Positioning emphasizes key profile sections: Headline, Summary/About, and Experience, as the algorithm values words in these sections. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- To identify necessary keywords, use Jobscan to compare your profile against ideal job postings. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Photo choice should match desired corporate culture (e.g., casual for startups, formal for finance). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Recommendations are preferred over endorsements for visibility. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
