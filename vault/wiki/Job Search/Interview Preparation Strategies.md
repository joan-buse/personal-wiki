# Interview Preparation Strategies

This section outlines methods for researching companies and preparing for interviews using LinkedIn features. It suggests using company language and searching for popular posts and groups to find industry insights.

## Details

- Read through a company’s page using their same language in interviews and resumes. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Search key words in the search bar, select “Posts” to find popular posts highlighting big names. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Follow “Linkedin influencers” to understand industry happenings and language. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Use the Job icon > Interview prep to see common practice questions. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Use Advanced search to find past employees and ask specific questions about leaving and returning. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
