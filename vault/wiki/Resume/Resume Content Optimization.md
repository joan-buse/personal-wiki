# Resume Content Optimization

Optimize resume content by looking for repeated words in job descriptions and tailoring your resume language to match the role's vocabulary.

## Details

- Look for repeated words in a target job description and repeat them in resume bullets *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Highlight experience with stakeholders if the posting mentions their customers *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop]])*
- For niche companies, pick attributes appealing to the target audience like revenue or customer size *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop]])*
