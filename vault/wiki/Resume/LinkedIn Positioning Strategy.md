# LinkedIn Positioning Strategy

Positioning on LinkedIn involves optimizing profile sections, especially the headline and summary, using keywords from dream job postings.

## Details

- The algorithm places high value on words in the Headline, Summary/About, and Experience sections due to character limits. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- The Headline should state your aspiration (e.g., 'Aspiring Product Manager'). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- The About section should include a summary of your experience and what you are looking for. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Use Jobscan to check the percentage of keywords from job descriptions reflected in your profile. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Photo attire must match the desired corporate culture. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
