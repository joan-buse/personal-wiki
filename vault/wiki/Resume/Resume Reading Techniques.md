# Resume Reading Techniques

This session advises looking for repeated words in job descriptions and incorporating them into resume bullets to match the role's language.

## Details

- Look for repeated words in a target job description and try to repeat those in your resume bullets *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- If the posting says “The successful candidate must commit to XYZ dates,” be ready to commit to these *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Really long phrases (bullets that are extra long or at a greater level of detail than the others) suggest this is a pain point in the company; network to confirm this is the case and emphasize the skills that can “fix” or combat this *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Most job descriptions are templates that hiring managers then tweak for the most important aspects of the role *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- If there is a phrase that is a-typical, it means it was intentionally changed *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
