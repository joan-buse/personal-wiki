# LinkedIn Networking Techniques

The source provides specific tactics for engaging with people on LinkedIn, including finding industry voices and connecting with former employees.

## Details

- Use company language when researching and applying. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Search for popular posts by selecting “Posts” and filtering by keywords. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Follow influencers to learn industry language and happenings. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Join groups based on function or role and message people with similar characteristics. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Contact former employees with specific questions about why they left and if they would work there again. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
