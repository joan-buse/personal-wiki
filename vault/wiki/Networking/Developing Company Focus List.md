# Developing Company Focus List

This section advises on how to develop a focused list of companies and industries for networking purposes.

## Details

- Define specific tech or fintech types. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Start with dream employers. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Look at conferences of interest to list attending companies and sponsors. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- CMG has seen success with student cold-calling dream companies. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
