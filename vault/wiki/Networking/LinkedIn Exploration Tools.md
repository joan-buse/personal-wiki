# LinkedIn Exploration Tools

LinkedIn offers specific tools for exploring industry and company information, including an alumni tool and cold outreach guidelines.

## Details

- Alumni tool allows searching by school name and filtering by desired function or company. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Cold outreach message structure suggests proving commonality, stating what you are looking for, and taking friction out of the request. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Cover photos can be sourced from royalty-free images on Pexels. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Example profiles can be found by searching '[School name] [Desired job title]'. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
- Recommendations are better than endorsements and should focus on quality connections. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training]])*
