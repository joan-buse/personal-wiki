# Networking Follow Up Strategy

The source recommends specific methods for following up after an initial networking interaction.

## Details

- Send a thank you note. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Keep in touch using a 'post card' strategy (notifying them of milestones). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
