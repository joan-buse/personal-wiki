# Networking Outreach Principles

These principles guide effective outreach when networking, focusing on brevity, connection, and focusing on the other person.

## Details

- Keep outreach short under 100 words. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Only ask for 15-20 minutes. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- State your connection upfront (e.g., Ex-Fintech to Fintech). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Generalize interest in industry/role. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Focus on the other person. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training]])*
