>   ●​ Linkedin cold outreach message guidelines (Commonality, Curiosity, and Call-to-Action)
>          ○​ Sentence #1: Prove you’re not a stranger, state what you have in common: “I’m a
>             current/alumni of (school), and I was thrilled to come across your profile.”
>          ○​ Sentence #2: State what you are looking for–learning, a referral, a job, etc: “I’m
>             torn between a PM and consulting career path and would love to learn from your
>             expertise.”
> ○​ Sentence #3: Take all the friction out of the process: “Any chance you have 10
>             minutes for a quick Zoom chat?”
