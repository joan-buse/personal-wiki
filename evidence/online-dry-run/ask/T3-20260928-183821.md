## Gemma answer

A networking outreach message should include elements such as proving you are not a stranger by stating what you have in common [S2]. A common structure for a cold outreach message involves three sentences: the first sentence should prove you share something in common, such as being a current or alumni of a school, and the second sentence should state what you are seeking, like learning from someone's expertise [S2]. The third sentence should take friction out of the process by asking for a brief time commitment, such as a 10-minute Zoom chat [S2]. Outreach should also be short, under 100 words, and only ask for 15-20 minutes [S3]. Furthermore, you should avoid mentioning looking for or asking for jobs or internships in the initial outreach [S3].
