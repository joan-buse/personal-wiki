# Successful Job Hunt Components

A successful job hunt involves five key elements: exploration, research, positioning, networking, and the job hunt itself.

## Details

- Exploration involves navigating the alumni tool for school name searches and filtering by desired function and company. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Positioning focuses on optimizing LinkedIn profile elements like Headline, Summary, and Experience. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Job Hunt involves creating alerts, toggling the 'Open to opportunities' button, and using search filters. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Networking involves connecting with second-degree connections and alumni. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Recruiters prefer referrals because referred candidates are hired faster and stay longer. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
