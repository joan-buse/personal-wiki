# Developing A Successful Job Search

A successful job search involves six steps, starting with clarity on your direction and ending with high-stakes testing and refinement.

## Details

- Steps include Desk research, Value proposition development, Low-to-medium stakes pressure test, High stakes pressure test, and final Revision and refinement *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
