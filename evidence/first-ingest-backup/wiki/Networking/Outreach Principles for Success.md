# Outreach Principles for Success

Effective outreach requires specific principles such as keeping messages short, stating the connection upfront, and focusing the conversation on the other person.

## Details

- Keep outreach short less than 100 words. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Only ask for 15-20 minutes. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- State your connection up front (e.g., Ex-Fintech to Fintech). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Generalize interest in industry/role. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- It’s about them. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
