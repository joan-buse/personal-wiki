# LinkedIn Networking Strategies

Effective networking on LinkedIn includes using personalized outreach messages for referrals and leveraging alumni connections.

## Details

- Cold outreach messages should follow a structure: Prove commonality, state what you are looking for, and take friction out of the process. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Second-degree connections can be leveraged by searching for mutual connections and sending messages to them. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Alumni outreach messages should mention shared college and ask for advice or a brief chat. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Example outreach message structure involves asking for a 10-minute chat to learn expertise. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
