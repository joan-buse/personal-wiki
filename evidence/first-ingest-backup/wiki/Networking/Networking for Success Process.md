# Networking for Success Process

This outlines a four-step process for effective networking, starting with generating a hypothesis and ending with planning the first outreach.

## Details

- Generate a hypothesis to explore industry function location etc. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Generate learning targets 40-50 target companies and look at earnings strategy etc. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Begin your networking list people from undergrad and grad who work there. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Plan first outreach. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
