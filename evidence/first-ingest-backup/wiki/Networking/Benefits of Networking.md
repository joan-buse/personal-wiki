# Benefits of Networking

Networking provides several benefits including refining targets, learning vocabulary, identifying hidden opportunities, and building relationships.

## Details

- Refine target industry and roles. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Learn the vocabulary. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Identify hidden opportunities (60% of jobs are not posted). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Practice and refine your personal pitch. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- The further in your career, the more likely the next job is from your network. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
