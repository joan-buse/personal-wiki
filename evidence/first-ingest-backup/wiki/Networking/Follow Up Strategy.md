# Follow Up Strategy

A good follow-up strategy involves sending thank you notes and keeping in touch through milestones or 'post card' strategies.

## Details

- Send a thank you note. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Keep them in the loop or use the post card strategy. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Notify them of milestones (e.g., finishing semester, coming back from a trip). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
