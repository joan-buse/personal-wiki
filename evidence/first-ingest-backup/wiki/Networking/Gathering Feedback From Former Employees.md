# Gathering Feedback From Former Employees

The source suggests using LinkedIn to find former employees for valuable, unfiltered feedback.

## Details

- Use All People > Advanced search > Past company [Name of company] to find former employees *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Example message to ask for a chat *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Essential questions to ask former employees are why they left and if they would work there again *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
