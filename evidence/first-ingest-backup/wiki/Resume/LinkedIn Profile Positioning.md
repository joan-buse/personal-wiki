# LinkedIn Profile Positioning

Positioning on LinkedIn involves strategically placing keywords in key sections and optimizing profile visuals to appeal to recruiters.

## Details

- Recruiters search by job title, key words, connection closeness, and photo. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Most important placement order is Headline, Summary/About, and Experience. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- The algorithm values words in the Headline, Summary/About, and Experience due to character limits. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- The About section should include one line about what you are and one line about what you want. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Photo attire should match the desired corporate culture (e.g., casual for startups, formal for finance). *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
- Use Jobscan to identify missing keywords by comparing your profile to dream job postings. *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Linkedin Training .pdf|Aug 2025   Linkedin Training]])*
