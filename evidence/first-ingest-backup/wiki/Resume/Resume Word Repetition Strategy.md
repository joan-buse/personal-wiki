# Resume Word Repetition Strategy

Look for repeated words in a target job description and incorporate them into your resume bullets.

## Details

- Highlight 'Creativity' as thinking outside the box *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Highlight 'Monetization' by stating what you monetized *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Highlight 'Cross-functional leadership' by mentioning working with multiple teams simultaneously *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Highlight customer experience if the posting mentions customers and you have experience with them *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
