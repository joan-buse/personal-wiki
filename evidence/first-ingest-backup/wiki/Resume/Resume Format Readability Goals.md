# Resume Format Readability Goals

The primary goal of resume formatting is readability, as recruiters spend only 7-8 seconds reviewing a resume.

## Details

- Chronological format is for classic industries like banking *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Hybrid format uses job titles followed by skill-based bullets *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Functional format is best for big pivots or unfamiliar industries *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
