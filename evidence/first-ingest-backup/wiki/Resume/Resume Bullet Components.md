# Resume Bullet Components

Effective resume bullets must include at least three elements: What, How, and Impact.

## Details

- Include What: The work object made *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Include How: The skills used to make the work object *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Include Impact: Measurable outcomes, ideally *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
- Include Why: Importance or frequency of the work *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume & Networking Training.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume & Networking Training]])*
