# Resume Content Removal Advice

Certain elements should be removed from a resume to improve its focus and readability.

## Details

- Remove undergrad GPA as it invites comparison *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Remove undergrad awards unless space allows *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
