# Resume Bullet Structure Elements

Resume bullets should include at least three elements: What, How, and Impact.

## Details

- What: The thing or 'work object' you made *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- How: The skills used to make the work object (tailored to the employer/role) *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Impact: Measurable outcome ideally *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Why: Why it was important or part of a company's bigger strategy *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
