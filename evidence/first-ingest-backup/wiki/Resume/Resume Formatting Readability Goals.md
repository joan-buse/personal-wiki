# Resume Formatting Readability Goals

The primary goal of resume formatting is readability because recruiters spend only 7-8 seconds per resume.

## Details

- Chronological format is for classic industries and single pivots *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Hybrid format places job titles under the company name with skill-based bullets *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
- Functional format is best for big pivots, unfamiliar industries, transferable skills, or whole careers in one industry *(source: [[raw/Aug 2025 - Resume Only Workshop.pdf|Aug 2025   Resume Only Workshop]])*
